REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN

From a live handshake to a defensible conclusion.

The workflow preserves target metadata, certificate evidence, protocol probes, cipher support, scores, and timestamps in one local database.

01

Define the dataset

A hostname-only list supports descriptive analysis. Add country and sector columns when a grouped comparison is required.

02

Collect full evidence

Validate trust, expiry, revocation, and signatures; test TLS 1.3 through SSL 2.0; measure DHE and cipher strengths.

03

Score consistently

Certificate quality contributes 40%; protocol, key exchange, and cipher strength form the 60% configuration score. Protocol checks without a definitive result are excluded. Partial cipher and DHE coverage receives proportional uncertainty deductions instead of suppressing the whole score.

04

Build a comparison

Select any two observed country or sector labels. The other label field is used as the stratification control.

SCORING MODEL

100-point grade

40Certificate
21Protocols
21Key exchange
18Ciphers

Critical trust, expiry, revocation, and hostname failures cap the final grade. Unknown trust deducts 8 certificate points; incomplete cipher and DHE coverage deduct up to 30 and 25 component points proportionally. Findings and remediation remain attached to every result.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION

SHA-1 fingerprint ≠ SHA-1 signature

The SHA-1 fingerprint is only a certificate identifier. The signature algorithm is evaluated separately; an actual SHA-1 or MD5 signature is penalised.

Results describe one point in time and one network location. CDN routing, transient failures, and inaccessible sites remain limitations.
